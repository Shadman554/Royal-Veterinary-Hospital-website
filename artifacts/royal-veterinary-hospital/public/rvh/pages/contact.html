VISITOR INFORMATION

What to Know Before Your Visit

Is parking available at the hospital?
Yes. We have dedicated client parking directly in front of the hospital entrance, including a designated emergency vehicle drop-off zone.
How should I transport my pet to the clinic?
For their safety and comfort, all cats and small mammals must arrive in a secure carrier box. Dogs must be on a leash. If your dog is anxious, please let our team know when you arrive so we can escort you directly to an examination room.
