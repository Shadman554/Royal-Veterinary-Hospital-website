Therapeutic Grooming & Spa

Professional grooming is not merely cosmetic—it is an essential facet of preventative skin and coat health. Our veterinary grooming salon provides hypoallergenic and medicated hydrotherapy baths, gentle brush-outs, sanitary trims, and nail clipping with patient, fear-free handling.

Clinical Scope & Inclusions

Every procedure at Royal Veterinary Hospital is performed with meticulous attention to detail and modern infection control.

Medicated Dermatological Baths

Customized veterinary shampoos targeting atopic dermatitis, fungal issues, dry skin, and flea control.

Gentle De-Shedding & Matting Relief

Careful removal of tangled fur that causes painful skin tension and moisture retention.

Nail & Paw Care

Precision claw clipping and pad conditioning to maintain natural foot posture and comfort.

Ear Canal Cleansing

Inspection and gentle cleansing of ear folds to deter chronic bacterial and yeast otitis.

PATIENT JOURNEY

What to Expect During Your Visit

A structured, compassionate experience designed to keep you informed and your pet relaxed.

01
Skin & Coat Evaluation

Assessment of fur condition, skin flaking, matting severity, and pet temperament.

02
Hydrotherapy Bath & Groom

Warm water massage bath, blow dry with variable temperature dryers, and precision styling.

03
Finishing Hygiene Touches

Nails trimmed, ears cleaned, coat deodorized with pet-safe conditioning mist.

Pre-Visit Preparation Guidelines:

Let our staff know if your pet has any sensitive skin conditions, arthritis, or prior fears regarding water or clippers.

PROCEDURE FAQs

Common Questions About Therapeutic Grooming

Do you groom cats?
Yes! Our groomers are specially trained in feline handling, ensuring quiet, calm, stress-free sessions for cats.
How often should a dog be groomed?
Most breeds benefit from professional grooming every 4 to 8 weeks depending on coat density and lifestyle.
