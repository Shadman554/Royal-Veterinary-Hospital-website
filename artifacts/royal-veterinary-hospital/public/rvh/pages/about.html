OUR STORY & VISION

Elevating Animal Healthcare in Kurdistan

Royal Veterinary Hospital was established with a singular, resolute mission: to provide the Kurdistan Region with an animal healthcare facility that meets international hospital standards in sterilization, anesthesia safety, diagnostic precision, and compassionate patient care.

Over the past decade, we have treated more than 12,500 animal patients. We have designed every square meter of our facility—from our separate canine and feline waiting zones to our positive-pressure surgical suite—to minimize patient stress and foster clinical excellence.

Whether your companion requires routine pediatric vaccination, emergency abdominal surgery, or gentle geriatric management, our team treats your pet with the same tenderness and precision we would demand for our own animals.
