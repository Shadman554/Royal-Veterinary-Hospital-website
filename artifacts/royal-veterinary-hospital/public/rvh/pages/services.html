COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about visiting our clinic, appointments, and medical standards.

Do I need to book an appointment or can I walk in?
We strongly recommend booking an appointment online or by phone to avoid waiting times for your pet. However, emergency trauma and critical cases are prioritized immediately at any time without appointment.
What diagnostic equipment do you have on-site?
We maintain an in-house IDEXX clinical laboratory providing CBC hematology, biochemistry, and blood gas analysis within 20 minutes, alongside digital high-frequency radiography (X-Ray), ultrasound, and microscopic cytology.
What payment methods are accepted at the hospital?
We accept Cash (IQD and USD), FastPay, FIB (First Iraqi Bank), ZainCash, and all major international Visa and Mastercard credit/debit cards.
