FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Package FAQs

Can I customize the tests included in a package?
Yes! During your consultation, our veterinarians can add optional diagnostic add-ons such as heartworm tests, digital dental cleanings, or abdominal ultrasounds at special member pricing.
Can I pay in Iraqi Dinar or US Dollars?
Yes. We accept both Iraqi Dinars (IQD) and US Dollars (USD), alongside mobile wallet transfers (FastPay, FIB, ZainCash) and bank cards.
