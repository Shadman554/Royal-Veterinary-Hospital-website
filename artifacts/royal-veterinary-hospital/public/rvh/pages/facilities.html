01 / 06 CLINICAL FACILITY

Consultation & Examination Suites

Our consultation suites are separated into dedicated feline and canine examination rooms. The feline room features calming Feliway pheromones, acoustic dampening, and non-slip padded surfaces to eliminate anxiety.

  • Separate feline and canine consultation spaces
  • Digital diagnostic display screens for pet parents
  • Hygiene sanitation between every patient visit

02 / 06 CLINICAL FACILITY

Positive-Pressure Surgical Suite

Constructed to international human hospital standards, our sterile surgical suite uses positive-pressure HEPA air handling, ceiling-mounted LED surgical lighting, and precision electrocautery to minimize surgical trauma and infection.

  • Isoflurane precision gas anesthesia machines
  • Continuous ECG, SpO2, blood pressure, and core temp telemetry
  • Autoclave steam sterilization of all instruments

03 / 06 CLINICAL FACILITY

In-House IDEXX Diagnostics & Radiology

On-site blood hematology, clinical chemistry, digital high-frequency radiography, and ultrasonography deliver critical diagnostic results in 15 to 20 minutes, allowing immediate intervention for acute conditions.

  • IDEXX Catalyst & ProCyte analyzers on-site
  • High-frequency low-dose digital X-Ray system
  • Real-time ultrasound for abdominal and cardiac imaging

04 / 06 CLINICAL FACILITY

Temperature-Controlled Recovery Wards

Post-surgical and hospitalized patients recover in quiet, temperature-regulated suites. Cats and dogs are housed in completely separate wards to ensure a peaceful, stress-free recovery environment.

  • Acoustically insulated recovery enclosures
  • Individual heat pads and IV infusion pumps
  • Continuous veterinary nurse monitoring
