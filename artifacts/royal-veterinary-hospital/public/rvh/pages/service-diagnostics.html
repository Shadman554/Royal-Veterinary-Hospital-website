DIAGNOSTICS SPECIALTY

In-House Laboratory & Digital Radiography

Accurate medical treatment depends on accurate diagnosis. Royal Veterinary Hospital maintains an advanced on-site laboratory and imaging center equipped with IDEXX analyzers and digital high-frequency radiography. Because we run diagnostics on-site, our doctors can initiate targeted therapies immediately.

WHAT IS INCLUDED

Clinical Scope & Inclusions

Every procedure at Royal Veterinary Hospital is performed with meticulous attention to detail and modern infection control.

Complete Blood Count & Chemistry

Rapid analysis of red/white cells, platelets, liver enzymes, kidney values, electrolytes, and blood glucose in 15 minutes.

High-Frequency Digital X-Ray

Crystal-clear skeletal and thoracic imaging with minimal radiation exposure and instant digital archive.

Diagnostic Ultrasound

Non-invasive abdominal imaging evaluating bladder, kidneys, liver, spleen, and pregnancy checks.

Microscopic Cytology & Urinalysis

Rapid evaluation of skin scrapings, ear swabs, needle aspirates, and urine specific gravity.

PATIENT JOURNEY

What to Expect During Your Visit

A structured, compassionate experience designed to keep you informed and your pet relaxed.

01
Clinical Indication

Veterinarian selects targeted diagnostic panels based on physical findings and pet symptoms.

02
Gentle Sample Collection

Blood, urine, or imaging performed with calm restraint and soothing assistance.

03
Immediate Doctor Review

Results analyzed in 20 minutes and reviewed directly with you on high-definition viewing monitors.

Pre-Visit Preparation Guidelines:

For accurate abdominal ultrasound, pets should ideally be fasted for 8 hours and have a full bladder if possible.

PROCEDURE FAQs

Common Questions About In-House Lab & X-Ray

How long do lab results take?
Our in-house clinical analyzers deliver complete blood and chemistry profiles in under 20 minutes during your visit.
Will I receive copies of X-rays?
Yes. All digital radiographic studies can be shared directly to your email or WhatsApp for your personal records.
